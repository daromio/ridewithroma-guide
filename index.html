Start here

Your journey starts with the wind.

You don't need previous board-sport experience to start learning kitesurfing. Lessons introduce you to kite control, wind awareness and water skills step by step, with safety as the priority.

01 / THE BASICS

What is kitesurfing?

Kitesurfing combines a controllable kite, a board and the power of the wind to move across the water. Beginners first learn to control the kite and understand the wind.

02 / YOUR FIRST SESSION

What happens in a lesson?

Your instructor introduces the equipment, wind window and safety systems before guiding you through kite control and practical water exercises suited to your progress.

03 / YOUR PROGRESS

How many lessons do you need?

Progress depends on wind, conditions, coordination and practice. Several hours may be enough to experience your first board rides, while riding independently usually takes more sessions.

04 / WHEN TO COME

Kitesurfing season in Tulum

The main riding season is generally November to May, but wind varies day to day. Check the forecast and confirm local conditions before planning a lesson.

Learn with confidence

Safety comes first.

Kitesurfing is a wind-dependent water sport. Choose qualified instruction, use appropriate equipment, learn the safety systems and assess local weather and water conditions before every session. Lessons should only take place when conditions are suitable.

Frequently asked questions

Before you book

Do I need to know how to surf?

No. Previous surfing experience is not required. Swimming ability and comfort in the water are important.

What should I bring?

Bring swimwear, sun protection and drinking water. Confirm the day's conditions and any additional recommendations with your instructor.
